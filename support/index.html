A note from me

Why I wrote it down

I've been writing this book on and off for nine years.

That meant going back through all of it. The house. The red recliner. Watching Lucia's kidneys give out. Her dad on the operating table. That room in January 2016. Some of it was hard to go back to. Some of it I'm not sure I'll ever be finished going back to.

I'm not a famous writer. I'm just a bloke from Concord who wanted to get it all down while I could still remember how it felt. And maybe, somewhere, there's someone sitting in a ward right now, or waiting on a scan, who reads it and feels a little less alone.

If you've read this far, thank you. Truly. And if you'd like to help me keep going, any amount means more than you'd think.

Nathan

A home of our own

At eighteen, I watched my family lose the house I grew up in. Lucia and I have been through a lot since, and now there are three kids in the picture. A first home of our own, somewhere for them to grow up, is still the dream.

Can't give right now? That's completely okay.

There are other ways to help, and every one of them matters to me.
