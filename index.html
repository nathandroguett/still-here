Still Here

by Nathan Droguett

The thing about a rollercoaster is you never see the drop coming. You're coasting along the top, wind in your face, grinning like an idiot. And then the track disappears beneath you.

At nineteen, I had cancer. A few months later, I met a girl on MySpace who had already died once, for a few minutes, when she was little. This is the next ten years of our lives. Thirty-four chapters, hundreds of real photos and videos, and every word of it true
